About DriveNow

Self-drive car rentals, made simple.

DriveNow is a self-drive car rental service operating across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune and Jaipur. We believe renting a car should be as easy as booking a cab — pick your city, choose your dates, and drive away.

  • 16 cars across 5 categories — hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, MPVs and luxury
  • Unlimited kilometres on every booking, no hidden charges
  • Transparent fares — GST and add-ons always shown before payment
  • Free cancellation until 24 hours before pickup

Note: DriveNow is a demo product built for software QA practice. No real cars, payments or accounts are involved.

How it works

1. Search

Pick your city and dates on the home page. Cars already booked for those dates are automatically hidden.

2. Book

Choose add-ons, apply a coupon if you have one, review the full fare breakdown, and pay (demo checkout).

3. Drive

Get your booking reference instantly. Manage or cancel your booking any time from My Bookings.
